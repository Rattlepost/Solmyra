Overview

Solmyra's pantheon is made up of eight gods, each pertianing to a specific domain. Each god has their own followers, and religious organizations that help spread their influence. The power of each god is directly related and sustained through the number of followers of that god. The greater the number of worshipers, the more powerful the god. This power share makes organized religion a necessity of the gods, but still puts them all at odds with each other. While their followers may interact cordially with each other, the gods all have rivalries working against one another in the background. If one member were to lose all followers, though its never happened, the consequences are assumed to be severe.
Name Domain Description
Vareth War and Conflict The most heavily worshiped domain and the most powerful of the gods. This domain gains its power from the disagreements between people.
Nyssara Ocean and Air This domain controls the ocean and the weather, bringing smooth sailing to followers and ruthless storms to those opposed.
Morfeld Death and Transition This domain focuses on the transitions of life, specifically from life to death. In Solmyra, death is a journey to a new place rather than an ending.
Irithee Knowledge and Secrets This domain oversees the accumulation of knowledge, and determines what should be shared or kept secret.
Corvane Harvest and Survival This domain ensures bountiful harvests and plentiful game for its worshipers. Those who do not follow find farming and hunting to not be their strong suit.
Stels Fate and Chance The domain of gambling. A bad hand is bound to lose, unless your opponent is unlucky.
Lyssae Beauty and Desire The domain of vanity, want, and ambition — for those who hope and dream of riches and beauty.
Thane Borders and Thresholds This domain governs liminal spaces and contracts. You will know if you have stepped out of your bounds.
