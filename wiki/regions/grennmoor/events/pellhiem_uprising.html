Pellhiem Uprising
Date 444 CE
Location Southern road
Participants Grennmoor, Pellhiem

Overview

The Pellhiem Uprising took place 196 years after the founding of Pellhiem. They raised a small strike force to try and capture Fenwick, Grennmoor's third largest city to the south. Grennmoor met Pellhiem's forces at the southern mountain road. Pellhiem's army was small, but so was Grennmoor's. The battle lasted several days and ended with both sides forces being almost completely wiped out with Grennmoor pushing back what little Pellhiem had left. After almost losing to the "tiny" Pellhiem nation, Grennmoor constructed Fort Perrol to keep watch for any future attacks.
