Stillwood Grove

Stillwood Grove
Location North and east of Stillhaven

Overview

The Stillwood Grove is north and east of Stillhaven, and stretches all the way to the sea. It is a massive, dark, and looming forest that sends chills down anyone's spine who looks into its quiet undergrowth. The locals never feel at ease around it, as if there is something wrong with the forest. The trees are too still and no animals can be seen or heard past the tree line. The quiet that emanates from the grove isn't the same peaceful quiet as the surrounding countryside; it is a quiet that presses upon those who listen. The residents avoid the forest entirely for reasons they cannot fully explain besides the constant unease that it brings them.
