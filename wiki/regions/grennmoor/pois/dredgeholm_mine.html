Location North of Dredgeholm

Overview

Dredgeholm mine is to the north of Murkmere lake just before the Stillwood Grove tree line. It is the primary source of metals and minerals for Grennmoor. It is a well constructed mine, being the only one in Grennmoor, and has around one hundred workers per day digging year round. The mine is deep and expansive, and very easy to get lost in if your unfamiliar with it.
