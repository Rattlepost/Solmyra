Overview

Greyport is the primary port city of Grennmoor and the kingdoms main passage to the sea, sitting at the northern Frontier Waters in the western edge of the region. It is a rather small city for a port, but medium in size compared to the rest of the region. It is functional but weathered from centuries of use, and the volume of trade that passes through the city tells a different story then the streets. The building are old and in need of repairs and the infrastructure has been maintained but not improved since its original construction. Unfortunately, the prosperity that passes through Greyport rarely is shared with it.
