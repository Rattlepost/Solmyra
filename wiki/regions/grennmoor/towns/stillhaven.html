Overview

Stillhaven sits at the cold northern edge of Grennmoor, far from the kingdoms political center. Only small towns can be found north of Stillhaven with the exception of Valdrest, a small town with a large castle of the rich lord that resides there. By all measures, Stillhaven should be a small, modest frontier settlement, not along the major trade routes to Duskhelm. However, it is the regions second largest city and inexplicably prosperous. The streets are wide for caravans and well cobbled. The buildings are solid stone and timber and kept in good repair. There are two functioning bathhouses, a covered market that operates year round, and a large inn, the Wayrest, that runs a cleaner and warmer establishment then any inns in Duskhelm. Trade comes to Stillhaven in volumes that have never been explained by its geographical location. In the winter, harsh storms make travel dangerous and in the summer, constant rain muddies the roads. But trade still finds its way as merchants take the northern road out of Greyport rather then the southern path to Duskhelm. They always have a sense for the good deals and full purses that come from Stillhaven, even foreigners. To the north and eastern edge of the town lies the Stillwood Grove. Dark and looming, it sends chills down anyone's spine who looks into the quiet undergrowth for too long. Locals do not go into it and don't talk much about it either, other then the occasional offhand remarks about its eerie silence. Travelers to the region barely notice it, and often don't remember the forest at all when they leave. The town seems to take your attention to other things, making you forget anything not worth remembering.

Ruler & Government

Stillhaven is governed by Lord Edran Cale, a human man in his late fifties who has governed the town for over two decades now. He is not disliked, keeps taxes fair, and settles disputes with minimal drama. Visitors can often meet him at the market or at one of the many restaurants in the city and often describe him as measured and hospitable man.
