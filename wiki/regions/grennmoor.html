Overview

Grennmoor is a human kingdom located in the Eastern Continent of Solmyra. It is known for its rich history, diverse culture, and strategic location. The kingdom has a strong military presence and is often involved in regional politics and trade. The capital city is a hub of commerce and culture, attracting merchants and scholars from across the continent. The people of Grennmoor are known for their resilience and adaptability, making it a significant player in the continent's affairs.
