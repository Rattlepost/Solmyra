Overview

Grennmoor is one of the oldest regions in Solmyra. Its capital is Duskhelm, a sprawling city with canals running through the neighborhoods. To its south lies Pellhiem, a rival nation of humans that split from Grennmoor many years ago. Tensions are rising between the two regions as rumors fly of Pellhiem bolstering is militant forces for a possible attack on Grennmoor. Grennmoor is a land rich with good soil for farming and cattle, but little in metals and minerals. For what little they do have, harvested from mines north of Dredgeholm, it goes to city projects in Duskhelm and rarely is it used for defense. Grennmoor is a proud nation, believing they do not need a standing army. A tiny nation like Pellhiem could never defeat them.

Geography

Grennmoor is in the northern half of Solmarch. It has cold forests to the north, rainy floodplains at its midlands, and mountains inhabited by wyrms to its south.

People & Culture

The people of Grennmoor are mostly humans, with the occasional visitor from other races. However, Duskhelm does not allow non-humans to settle permanently. Many different religions are practiced throughout the region and tend to coexist peacefully.

History

Grennmoor's history spans nearly 500 years. It was founded by a group of humans in 1 CE who sailed across the sea from Marevoss to Solmarch. They met the shore in the frontier waters where the Midwoods are today, traveled inland, and settled where Duskhelm now stands. Early attempts to cross the mountains to the south were met with resistance from the wyrms that lived there as well as harsh weather conditions. No attempts were made until the 2nd century, when Duskhelm decided to build a fort in the mountians for harvesting minerals and metals. The fort was abandoned after two years due to the frequent wyrm attacks because of the fort's hoard of metals.

Notable Locations

  • Duskhelm — The capital city of Grennmoor, known for its canals and sprawling neighborhoods.
  • Greyport — The main port city of Grennmoor, located on the northwest coast.
  • Stillhaven — The second largest city in Grennmoor, known for its prosperous markets and hospitality.
  • Fort Perrol — A military fort on the southern road, Grennmoor's first line of defense against Pellhiem.
  • Midlands — The farming region of Grennmoor. Rainy and fertile in the spring and summer, this is where most of the agriculture takes place.
