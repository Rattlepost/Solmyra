Overview

The Far Lands are a region of four islands to the far west of Solmyra. No vessel in recorded history has ever successfully reached them and lived to tell the tale. Those who have attempted and returned describe the area around the islands as though it is against them. Compasses behave strangely, navigation by sky fails, and distances are not as expected. The islands are not mentioned in any serious literature or geographical depictions. They are rather considered the end of the world.
