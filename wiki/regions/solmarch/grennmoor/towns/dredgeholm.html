Overview

Dredgeholm lies on the shores of Murkmere lake. It is medium sized mining town, Grennmoor's largest and basically only source of metal and minerals. To the north of the city lies an outpost for miners to get gear and food before heading off the the mine north of the outpost. They also run sift metals from the lake, Murkmere, during the spring when the rainfall brings new sediments down from the mountains. Its a fairly quiet town, ruled over by Lord Ferno Balish, a quiet man focused on doing his assigned job from Duskhelm, get metals and minerals for the capital.
