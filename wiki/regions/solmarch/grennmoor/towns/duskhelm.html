Overview

Duskhelm is the oldest and largest city in Grennmoor, acting as the political epicenter for the region. Centuries of construction have accumulated layers upon layers of stone in a dense urban sprawl. Canals line many neighborhoods; an effective solution for the frequent flooding that occurs from the spring rains in the midlands of Grennmoor. Throughout the warmer months, boats travel these canals bringing goods and people around the city. The great castle in the city center towers over the surrounding neighborhoods. It is a fortified complex that has grown over the centuries with several additions and renovations, with its older structures distinctly darker than the newer ones. This place is where the Council of Lords resides, convenes, and deliberates. It gives Duskhelm a certain atmosphere, born from the weight of its own politics, noticed by all who visit and live there. The city exists in the shadow of its governance, often filtering down into the lives of everyday people from the price of goods to the moods of its residents. The council is a slow churning machine, rarely getting anything of substance done; an artifact of an old and unchanged governmental system. However, their patience had been sharpened recently as Pellhiem grows bolder to their south. The last attack from the rouge nation was almost 70 years ago, near the location of Fort Perrol, but now it seems they may be planning again. The only problem, Duskhelm does not keep a large standing army. Metal is in the mountains to the south but is unharvestable because of the wyrms that call them home. For this reason, it is hard to arm non magic users with weapons and armor for battle. The warriors that do exist either roam Duskhelm as it police force or are privately hired by lords of each small town. Now the council must bolster its defenses, but to do so requires agreement between six council members; a rare occurrence even in times of peace. The Military wants resources, Diplomacy wants a table, and the Treasury wants neither. The debates drag on as the pressure builds from south.

Ruler & Government

The Council of Duskhelm is the governing body of the city and the wider Grennmoor region. It is comprised of nine lords, each responsible for representing a specific portfolio of governance. The nine seats are commerce, agriculture, military, justice, infrastructure, treasury, diplomacy, clergy, and lands. There is no head of the council, they all have equal say in what gets passed for the region. Any lord may bring a matter to the floor each day, dictated by how recently they have brought an issue forward. Whoever has gone longest without bringing an issue to the floor may go first and so on. Then the matter is deliberated on for the day until a verdict is reach or, if one has not been reached by the end of the day, a vote is had to close the deliberations. To pass anything, six or more lords must agree to pass it. However, this is a rare occurrence. Most days are filled with disagreements and shouting. And since each lord usually only gets to bring forward one issue every nine days, and not much productive happens in the council chamber, an issue may take months to pass.
