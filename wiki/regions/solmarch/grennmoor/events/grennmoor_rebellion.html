Grennmoor Rebellion

Grennmoor Rebellion
Date 376 CE
Location Duskhelm
Participants Grennmoor, Pell's Rebels

Overview

Grennmoor was in a time of unrest. A spring of heavy rainfall made the fields flood more than usual, destroying this years harvest. The Council of Duskhelm was slow to address the issue, as always, and this lead to economic strain and hunger spread throughout the region. As the starvation spread and more people died from it, the southern towns got more and more resentful of the capital. Eventually, an uprising was organized by Brennic Pell, a member of a minor Duskhelm royal family, but not important enough to have an impact. Pell gathered together any private mercenaries and people who felt the same and attacked Duskhelm's central castle. Unfortunately, the guards who had been paid off did not allow them through the gates as promised, stopping it in its tracks. All who participated in the uprising fled the region through the southern mountains to avoid punishment. The path was dangerous but most survived the trip. On the other side of the mountains they found a large lake in a valley and settled there. Over the next twenty years, more and more people fled Grennmoor and migrated to the new city. The new city thrived and expanded into a large sprawling city-state. It became known as Pellhiem, named after the man who had the courage to stand up to Duskhelm, even though he ultimately failed.
