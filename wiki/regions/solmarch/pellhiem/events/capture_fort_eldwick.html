Capture of Fort Eldwick

Capture of Fort Eldwick
Date 442 CE
Location Within the Hellspur Mountains
Participants Pellhiem, Wyrm

Overview

Fort Eldwick was abandoned by Grennmoor shortly after its construction. Forty years later, the Grennmoor Rebellion forced a group of Gremorians to flee and create the region of Pellhiem. Later into Pellhiem's years, they needed new sources of metal. Fort Eldwick was not talked about much in Grennmoor as it was seen as a failure and not worth discussing; more of a rumor then fact. The location was only generally known, so Pellhiem organized multiple search parties. The first party went down the north road, where the fort was rumored to be. Unfortunately, a late spring winter storm caught them off guard and killed the entire party. The second party tried the southern road during the summer months where it is slightly warmer. They were not successful in finding the fort, but they did find an unguarded border or Grennmoor. They returned with their findings and a third search party was sent out. They went down the northern road in the early fall months, hoping to do better then the previous party. After five weeks of searching, what appeared to be an old and decrepit road led around the mountains, a place they had not been before. After walking the road, they came upon the fort hidden in the hills. It's walls had mostly fallen, only the southern facing wall was still standing. The base of the fort seemed to still be intact and sturdy, surprising for a structure so old. They ventured into the fort and inspected what was left. After confirming it was in fact safe, they celebrated, playing music and singing, while a celebratory meal was prepared. What they did not know however, a wyrm had called this place home for the last forty years. The loud music and singing brought the flying creature home without warning. Two party members were killed almost instantly before anyone could react to what had happened. The wyrm then landed in the fort and a battle was had. Thankfully, three skilled fighters had come with the party along with a cleric. With their efforts, the wyrm was killed before any other party members died. After killing the wyrm, they hung its skeleton on the southern wall as a warning to other wyrms, and it worked. Fort Eldwick became the main mining operation for Pellhiem shortly after the party returned with their victory, and the fort has not since been attacked by another wyrm.
