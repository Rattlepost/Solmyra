Fort Eldwick

Fort Eldwick
Location Within the Hellspur Mountains
Constructed 206 CE

Overview

Fort Eldwick was originally constructed in 206 CE by Grennmoor as an outpost dedicated to mining minerals and metals from within the mountains. However, wyrms living in the mountains began terrorizing the outpost weekly because of the shiny objects collected there. This made the fort almost impossible to use and was abandoned shortly after its construction two years later. Over two hundred years later, the structure was reclaimed by a small Pellhiem army. Although it was in disrepair, Pellhiem was still able to use it as a mining base of operations. To scare off wyrm attacks, they hung the skeleton of a wyrm defeated upon retaking the base on the fort wall. They were no longer attacked by wyrms after that.
