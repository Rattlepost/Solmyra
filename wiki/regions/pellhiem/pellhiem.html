Overview

Pellhiem was founded after the Grennmoor Rebellion, lead by Brennic Pell. It is a sprawling city-state next to a massive valley lake. The lake helps grow crops while the mountains to the north provide natural protection against Grennmoor. Pellhiem is ruled by a royal family, the bloodline of Brennic Pell, believed to be blessed by Vareth. It is a monarchy, having a king or queen pass judgement down for the whole city-state. Its not a perfect government, but at least things get done, good or bad, unlike the deadlock Grennmoor is always stuck in. The current ruler is Queen Darka Pell, a bold leader who believes they should not have to live in isolation from the world and wants Grennmoor's ports, so they can finally be free.

Geography

Pellhiem is in the southern half of Solmarch. It has a large valley lake to the northwest, mountains along the northern border, a forest to the south, and a dry plains to the east and southeast.

People & Culture

The people of Pellhiem are mostly humans, with the occasional visitor from other races. Pellhiem does not have a large population, but it is a proud one. They have a strong military culture, often worshiping Vareth, the god of War and Conflict. They are loyal to their land and will fight those that oppose them, mainly Grennmoor.

History

After the rebellion, Pellhiem amassed an army and captured Fort Eldwick, built by Grennmoor but ultimately abandoned because of frequent wyrm attacks. With this fort, they were able to start amassing tons of metal and minerals inaccessible by Grennmoor in the mountains, making them a wealthy country. Unfortunately, they had no port to Dawnmere, meaning they could not sell any of this newfound wealth. Instead, they put it to use crafting weapons and armor. Pellhiem now has a massive amount of weapons and armor at its disposal, more then its army could every use, and they intend to bring it to a fight with Grennmoor once they recruit enough brave souls. After capturing the fort, Pellhiem decided to try and take on Grennmoor at is southern border. This battle failed, wiping out both sides armies almost completely. After this defeat, Pellhiem retreated and did not seek out conflict for generations after.
