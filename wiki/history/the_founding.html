The Founding

The Founding of Solmyra

The origins of Solmyra and its inhabitants came about in the year 5000 BCE. There were two primordial beings, Order and Chaos. These beings were not opposing forces but rather joined in their efforts to keep the world unified. As a part of this union and stability, the primordials created the first intelligent inhabitants of Solmyra, the Vaer.

The Vaer

The Vaer were a pre-humanoid race that occupied the region now known as Penta. They were reptilian in appearance. Tall, lanky, bipedal creatures that were extremely gifted in mental prowess, magical ability, and architectural instinct in addition to extremely long life spans lasting multiple centuries. They were also profoundly arrogant as a species and viewed the Primordials not as their benevolent creators but as obstacles to their research in magic and science, always setting their reality altering mistakes right. This lead the Vaer to pursue a project known as the Sealing Ritual.

The Sealing Ritual

The Sealing Ritual was a project undertaken by the eight highest ranking Vaer to seal away the primordials with magic. The goal of this project was to remove the primordials of enough power to trap them in a magical prison, unable to meddle with the Vaer's projects anymore. However the Ritual did not go exactly as planned and led to the cataclysm of Penta.

The Cataclysm of Penta

The upon completion of the sealing ritual an immense amount of raw magic was released from the Primordials. Some of this magic was absorbed by the eight Vaer conducting the ritual. These eight became divine beings with the magic absorbed. The remaining magic resulted in a massive explosion that decimated the entire region of Penta. All traces of the civilization they once had were wiped away in an instant along with all the Vaer people who had gathered for this great achievement. However, the Vaer people did not die. Instead, they were cosmically ripped apart and remade into the different humanoid races that are known today. Each race taking a part of the Vaer with them, but not the whole. Humans their ego and ambition, dwarves their architectural instinct, elves their longevity and perceptiveness. These new races dispersed across Solmyra and slowly forgot their origins over thousands of years. In the present day, there is no trace of the Vaer through culture, writing, architecture, or song. Only the faint echoes of Solmyra's ancestors shining through people's very nature, yet unrecognizable to any of them.
